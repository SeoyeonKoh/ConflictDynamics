## Non-evidence

- No source above establishes the actual reporting lines, team size, room allocation, authority graph, or personality mix of Samsung Electronics.
- Persona names, hobbies, relationships, project details, tick duration, and event parameters are synthetic assumptions.
- Public company material is used for plausibility, not for representing a digital twin.

## Recency audit

The 2026 official report index and current Facts & Figures were checked on 2026-09-25. They
continue to publish broad job/rank categories but do not publish the team-level reporting,
authority, DISC, or staffing ratios modeled here. Nothing found reverses the narrower public
statements in S02 or S03; those older sources are retained only for the specific policies they
announced, not treated as a complete description of current internal practice.
